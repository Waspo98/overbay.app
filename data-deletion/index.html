Data & Account Deletion

Developer: Neal Overbay Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Support: privacy@overbay.app
Overview: I don't host user accounts or cloud databases for Luma Reader, AllNotes, Orbit Money, or Overbay Labs. You control your phone's storage, your Google Drive app data, and any server you host yourself.

1. Delete Local Android App Data (All Apps)

To wipe local books, notes, budgets, cached covers, and settings stored on your phone by Luma Reader, AllNotes, Orbit Money, or Twitter (Overbay Labs):

  1. Open Android Settings > Apps and select the app.
  2. Tap Storage & cache > Clear storage (or just uninstall the app).

Because I don't hold a copy of your local files, clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app's database from your phone while leaving any original EPUBs, PDFs, or CSV files in your Downloads folder untouched.

2. Revoke Google Drive Access & Delete Synced Data (Luma Reader & Orbit Money)

If you connected your Google Account in Luma Reader (drive.appdata) or Orbit Money (drive.appdata / drive.file):

  1. Sign out inside the app's Settings screen first if it's still installed.
  2. Open Google Account Third-Party Connections and remove access for Luma Reader or Orbit Money.
  3. To wipe hidden app data in your Google Drive, go to drive.google.com > Settings > Manage apps, find Luma Reader or Orbit Money, click Options > Delete hidden app data, and delete any visible Orbit Money backup files if you want.

3. Delete Data on Self-Hosted Servers (AllNotes & Orbit Money)

If you linked AllNotes to a self-hosted sync server or ran Orbit Money in Docker (ghcr.io/waspo98/orbit-money):

  • Remove the server connection inside the app Settings.
  • Stop the container on your computer or NAS and delete its mapped data folder (for example ./orbit-data).

4. Support Emails & Deletion Help

If you've emailed me before and want your messages deleted from my inbox, email privacy@overbay.app from the same address. I'll clear them out within 14 days.
