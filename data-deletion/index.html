Data & Account Deletion

Developer: Overbay Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Support: privacy@overbay.app
Overview: Overbay does not host central user accounts or central cloud databases for Luma Reader, AllNotes, Orbit Money, or Overbay Labs. You remain directly in control of your local device files, your Google Drive app data, and any server you self-host.

1. Delete Local Android App Data (All Apps)

To immediately erase all local books, notes, budgets, cached thumbnails, and preferences stored on your Android device by Luma Reader, AllNotes, Orbit Money, or Twitter (Overbay Labs):

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps and select the Overbay app.
  2. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage (or uninstall the application).

Because Overbay holds no shadow copy of your local files, clearing storage or uninstalling permanently deletes the local database from your phone (while leaving your original external source files untouched if you imported from Downloads).

2. Revoke Google Drive Access & Delete Synced Data (Luma Reader & Orbit Money)

If you optionally connected your Google Account in Luma Reader (drive.appdata) or Orbit Money (drive.appdata and/or drive.file):

  1. Sign out inside the app's Settings screen first if still installed.
  2. Visit Google Account Third-Party Connections and remove access for Luma Reader or Orbit Money.
  3. To purge hidden app data stored inside your Google Drive, open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps, locate Luma Reader or Orbit Money, click Options → Delete hidden app data, and remove any visible Orbit Money snapshot files if desired.

3. Delete Data on Self-Hosted Instances (AllNotes & Orbit Money)

If you linked AllNotes to a self-hosted Ktor sync endpoint or deployed Orbit Money via Docker (ghcr.io/waspo98/orbit-money):

  • Remove the server profile inside the mobile/desktop app Settings.
  • Stop the self-hosted container on your machine or NAS and delete its mapped SQLite volume directory (for example ./orbit-data).

4. Support Emails & Deletion Assistance

If you previously contacted Overbay by email and want your correspondence deleted from our support mailbox, email privacy@overbay.app from the address used. Verified email deletion requests are processed within 14 days.
