A self-hosted Rocket Money alternative

I built Orbit Money because I wanted my spending, monthly budgets, recurring bills, and net worth in one place without paying a monthly fee or keeping years of bank transactions on a third-party server.

About Orbit Money Download Android APK Source on GitHub

Orbit Money is personal bookkeeping software only, not a bank, lender, or financial advisor.

Where Orbit Money matches Rocket Money, and the paid concierge features it skips.

What it replaces and what it skips

Most people leaving Rocket Money or Mint aren't looking for someone to call their cable company. They just want a quick view of where their money went this month, what bills are coming up, and how their net worth is moving.

If you want bill negotiation or someone to cancel subscriptions for you, stick with a paid service. If you just want a private budget tracker with no ads or monthly fee, Orbit Money covers that.

  • Original bank rows never get overwritten

    When you rename a cryptic card charge or recategorize a split, Orbit Money saves your edit alongside the original bank description, so re-importing CSVs later won't clobber your fixes.

  • Exact-cent math

    Every balance, budget, and split is stored in exact integer cents rather than floating-point decimals, so totals never drift by a penny.

  • Your SimpleFIN key stays on your hardware

    If you want automatic bank feeds in self-hosted mode, Orbit Money encrypts your SimpleFIN Bridge access URL with AES-256-GCM inside your own SQLite database. I never see or relay your token.

  • No account required to try it

    Start standalone on Android with everything saved locally, or spin up one Docker container at home when you want browser access and shared household logins.

Switch from Rocket Money in 3 steps

  1. Export your Rocket Money CSV

    Download your transaction history CSV from Rocket Money so you keep years of spending history, categories, and balances when you leave.

  2. Pick phone-only or Docker self-hosting

    Install the Android APK for offline budgeting (with optional fingerprint lock and Google Drive backups), or run the Docker Compose stack on any Linux box, Raspberry Pi, or NAS on port 5008.

  3. Import your CSV & optionally add SimpleFIN

    Use Orbit Money's Rocket Money CSV importer to bring in your history. In self-hosted Docker mode, you can also attach a SimpleFIN Bridge token for automated bank updates.

compose.yaml 
services:
  orbit-money:
    image: ghcr.io/waspo98/orbit-money:latest
    container_name: orbit-money
    ports:
      - "5008:5008"
    environment:
      - AUTH_PROVIDER=local
      - DATA_DIR=/data
    volumes:
      - ./orbit-data:/data
    restart: unless-stopped

How it fits next to Actual Budget & Firefly III

There are great open-source finance projects already out there, and picking the right one mostly comes down to how you like to think about budgeting.

  • Choose Actual Budget if you want strict envelope budgeting

    Actual is fast, local-first, and built around zero-based envelope budgeting (similar to YNAB).

  • Choose Firefly III if you want double-entry accounting

    Firefly III is mature and deeply configurable if you want strict accounting ledgers, complex automation rules, and granular reports.

  • Choose Orbit Money for a Rocket Money-style dashboard + native Android

    Orbit Money focuses on monthly budgets, recurring bills, net worth, household sharing, direct Rocket Money CSV import, and a native offline Android app that works without a server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import my Rocket Money transaction history into Orbit Money?

Yes. Orbit Money includes a Rocket Money CSV importer. Imported rows keep the original bank description alongside any merchant or category edits you make later, so re-importing CSVs won't wipe out your edits.

Does Orbit Money automatically sync with banks like Rocket Money?

When self-hosted in Docker, Orbit Money supports optional bank syncing through SimpleFIN Bridge. Your SimpleFIN access URL is encrypted with AES-256-GCM inside your own SQLite database, with no middleman server.

Can Orbit Money cancel subscriptions or negotiate bills for me?

No. Orbit Money tracks recurring bills and monthly budgets so you can spot unwanted charges quickly, but it doesn't call providers to negotiate bills or cancel subscriptions on your behalf.

Do I need a home server or Docker to leave Rocket Money for Orbit Money?

No. You can run Orbit Money offline on Android with biometric lock and optional private Google Drive backups, or spin up the Docker container later if you want multi-device household sync and SimpleFIN bank feeds.
