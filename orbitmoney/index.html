Two storage modes, same ledger

You shouldn't have to set up a server just to track a budget on your phone, and you shouldn't be stuck on one phone if you want to share expenses with a partner. Orbit Money supports both.

When you rename messy bank merchant strings or recategorize purchases, Orbit Money keeps the original original_* import fields intact alongside your edited_* labels so later CSV imports never clobber your edits.

  • On-Device Local Mode (Android & PWA)

    Keeps your full ledger locally on your phone or browser. Lock opening the app with Android's BiometricPrompt (USE_BIOMETRIC), and optionally back up JSON snapshots to your own Google Drive via drive.appdata or drive.file.

  • Self-Hosted Household Server (Docker + SQLite)

    Run ghcr.io/waspo98/orbit-money at home for shared household logins (AUTH_PROVIDER=local|oidc|both), recurring expense tracking, and multi-device syncing over orbitmoney:// or HTTPS.

  • Rocket Money CSV & Optional SimpleFIN

    Import historical CSV exports from Rocket Money out of the box, or link a SimpleFIN Bridge token in self-hosted mode—stored AES-256-GCM encrypted in your own SQLite file.

  • Optional Merchant Icons & Web Push

    Self-hosted instances can optionally query Logo.dev for merchant icons or send VAPID budget alerts when configured by the server administrator.

Self-host with Docker Compose

Run Orbit Money on any Linux server, NAS, or home box with a single persistent SQLite volume on port 5008.

See the README on GitHub for OIDC provider setup, SimpleFIN encryption key generation, and backup scripts.

compose.yaml 
services:
  orbit-money:
    image: ghcr.io/waspo98/orbit-money:latest
    container_name: orbit-money
    ports:
      - "5008:5008"
    environment:
      - AUTH_PROVIDER=local
      - DATA_DIR=/data
    volumes:
      - ./orbit-data:/data
    restart: unless-stopped

Frequently asked questions

Can I run Orbit Money completely offline on Android without Docker?

Yes. Orbit Money runs standalone on Android with local storage, optional BiometricPrompt lock, and optional JSON snapshot backups to your Google Drive account.

Why integer cents instead of standard decimals?

Floating-point arithmetic produces penny rounding errors across hundreds of transfers and splits. Storing values internally as integer cents (for example, $12.34 as 1234) keeps balances exact down to the cent.

Does Overbay have access to my bank credentials or SimpleFIN tokens?

No. Overbay runs no hosted Orbit Money servers. In self-hosted mode, your SimpleFIN Access URL stays encrypted with AES-256-GCM inside your own SQLite database, and Rocket Money CSV imports are parsed locally.

What Android permissions does Orbit Money declare?

Only INTERNET (for optional Google Drive sync or connecting to your own Docker server), ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, USE_BIOMETRIC (app lock), and VIBRATE (haptic feedback).

How do I delete my Orbit Money data?

Uninstall or clear storage on Android, delete snapshots in your Google Drive if Drive backup was enabled, or stop and remove your Docker /data volume. Step-by-step instructions live on the Data Deletion page.
