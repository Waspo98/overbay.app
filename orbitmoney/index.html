Two storage modes, same clean ledger

Not everyone wants to run a home server just to log groceries, and not everyone wants their household budget stuck on a single phone. Orbit Money supports both cleanly.

When you tidy up bank merchant names or split receipts, Orbit Money preserves immutable original_* bank columns next to your edited_* labels so re-importing CSV statements never overwrites your cleanup work.

  • On-Device Local Mode (Android & PWA)

    Keeps your full ledger locally on your device. Protect opening the app with Android's BiometricPrompt (USE_BIOMETRIC), and optionally sync JSON snapshots through your own Google Drive drive.appdata and drive.file permissions.

  • Self-Hosted Household Server (Docker + SQLite)

    Deploy ghcr.io/waspo98/orbit-money at home for shared household logins (AUTH_PROVIDER=local|oidc|both), recurring expense tracking, and multi-device browser/Android syncing via the orbitmoney:// app scheme.

  • Rocket Money CSV & Optional SimpleFIN

    Import historical CSV exports from Rocket Money out of the box, or configure a SimpleFIN Bridge token in self-hosted mode — stored AES-256-GCM encrypted in your own SQLite file.

  • Optional Merchant Icons & Web Push

    Self-hosted instances can optionally query Logo.dev for merchant icons or send VAPID budget alerts if configured by the server administrator. Both remain strictly opt-in.

Self-host with Docker Compose

Spin up Orbit Money on any Linux box, Synology NAS, or home server with a single persistent SQLite volume on port 5008.

See the repository README on GitHub for OIDC provider setup, SimpleFIN encryption key generation, and backup scripts.

compose.yaml 
services:
  orbit-money:
    image: ghcr.io/waspo98/orbit-money:latest
    container_name: orbit-money
    ports:
      - "5008:5008"
    environment:
      - AUTH_PROVIDER=local
      - DATA_DIR=/data
    volumes:
      - ./orbit-data:/data
    restart: unless-stopped

Frequently asked questions

Why integer cents instead of standard decimals?

Floating-point arithmetic produces penny rounding errors across hundreds of transfers and splits. Storing values internally as integer cents (for example, $12.34 as 1234) keeps balances exact down to the cent.

What Android permissions does Orbit Money declare?

Only INTERNET (for optional Google Drive sync or connecting to your own Docker server), ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, USE_BIOMETRIC (app lock), and VIBRATE (haptic feedback).

How do I delete my Orbit Money data?

Uninstall or clear storage on Android, delete snapshots in your Google Drive if Drive backup was enabled, or stop and remove your Docker /data volume. Step-by-step instructions live on our Data Deletion page.
