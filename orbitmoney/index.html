Use it on your phone—or host it at home

I built Orbit Money because you shouldn't have to set up a server just to track a budget on your phone, and you shouldn't be stuck on one phone if you share expenses with a partner. It works both ways.

When you clean up ugly bank merchant names or recategorize purchases, Orbit Money keeps the original bank description tucked away underneath—so importing new CSVs later never overwrites your edits.

  • Offline on Android & Web

    Keeps your budget right on your phone or browser. Lock the app with your fingerprint or face, and optionally save backups to your own Google Drive.

  • Home server for your household

    Spin up the Docker container (ghcr.io/waspo98/orbit-money) at home for shared logins and multi-device syncing across phones and laptops.

  • Rocket Money CSV & optional SimpleFIN

    Import your history from Rocket Money CSV exports right out of the box, or connect a SimpleFIN Bridge token in self-hosted mode—stored encrypted in your own database.

  • Optional extras

    Self-hosted instances can optionally turn on merchant brand icons (Logo.dev) or browser push notifications for budget alerts.

Self-host with Docker Compose

Run Orbit Money on any Linux box, Raspberry Pi, or NAS with one data folder on port 5008.

Check the README on GitHub for login options, SimpleFIN bank sync setup, and backup tips—or read the Rocket Money migration guide.

compose.yaml 
services:
  orbit-money:
    image: ghcr.io/waspo98/orbit-money:latest
    container_name: orbit-money
    ports:
      - "5008:5008"
    environment:
      - AUTH_PROVIDER=local
      - DATA_DIR=/data
    volumes:
      - ./orbit-data:/data
    restart: unless-stopped

Frequently asked questions

Is Orbit Money a self-hosted alternative to Rocket Money or Mint?

Yes, for tracking accounts, budgets, recurring bills, spending categories, and net worth. Orbit Money includes a direct Rocket Money CSV importer and optional SimpleFIN Bridge bank sync when self-hosted in Docker. Unlike Rocket Money, it does not negotiate bills or cancel subscriptions for you—you keep your data private and cancel subscriptions yourself. See the Rocket Money migration guide for details.

Can I run Orbit Money completely offline on Android without Docker?

Yes. It runs standalone on Android with everything saved on your phone, optional fingerprint/face unlock, and optional backups to your own Google Drive.

Do you have access to my bank info or transactions?

No. I don't run any servers for Orbit Money. Rocket Money CSV imports stay local, and if you link SimpleFIN Bridge in self-hosted mode, your access URL stays encrypted inside your own SQLite database.

Does Orbit Money round off pennies?

No. Balances and splits are stored in exact cents under the hood rather than imprecise decimals, so totals stay accurate down to the penny.

What permissions does the Android app ask for?

Only internet access (if you turn on Google Drive backups or connect your own Docker server), fingerprint/face lock, and vibration for button feedback.

How do I delete my Orbit Money data?

Clear app storage or uninstall on Android, delete backups in Google Drive if you turned them on, or remove your Docker data folder. Full steps live on the Data Deletion page.
