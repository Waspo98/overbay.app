Core apps

Apps I made for reading, notes, budgeting, and browsing. No forced accounts and no monthly fees.

Luma Reader

Most ebook apps either push a storefront or lock progress sync behind a monthly subscription. Luma Reader opens your own EPUBs and PDFs offline, formats two pages side by side on foldables, and reads aloud with Android's built-in voices while your screen is locked.

  • Works offline with your own EPUB and PDF files
  • Syncs reading progress and bookmarks through your private Google Drive
  • Free for 5 books, or $4.99 once for unlimited
About Luma Reader Privacy Policy

AllNotes

OneNote nailed how notebooks should be organized (notebooks, section groups, and tabs), but its endless 2D canvas gets annoying fast on a phone. AllNotes keeps that hierarchy and wraps every page to fit your screen.

  • Headings, checklists, callout boxes, tables, and code blocks
  • Pages wrap to your screen with no sideways scrolling
  • Stored locally on your device with no sign-in required

Orbit Money

You shouldn't have to set up a server just to budget on your phone, or hand years of bank transactions to a paid cloud service. Run Orbit Money offline on Android with fingerprint lock, or host it in Docker with Rocket Money CSV import and optional SimpleFIN bank sync.

  • Exact to-the-cent math so totals never drift
  • Cleaning up messy bank merchant names never overwrites the original
  • Optional Docker server for shared household budgets and bank sync

Swiftfox

I wanted Zen Browser and Firefox's Spaces, sync, and ad blocking on my foldable without jittery scrolling text. Swiftfox keeps separate folded and unfolded layouts, zaps sticky popups, darkens websites in AMOLED mode, and syncs tabs through your Mozilla Account.

  • Blocks ads and popups, plus Dark Reader and .user.js scripts
  • Separate folded and unfolded toolbars that don't reload your tabs
  • Two-way Firefox & Zen Browser sync plus local Chrome bookmark import
