Core apps

Reading, notes, and budgeting without forced accounts or monthly subscriptions.

Luma Reader

Open your own EPUBs and PDFs without a bookstore in the way. Customize fonts, spacing, and themes (Light, Sepia, Dark, or AMOLED), read two pages side by side on foldables, or have Android read out loud while your screen is locked.

  • Works completely offline with your own EPUB and PDF files
  • Syncs your reading spot and bookmarks via your private Google Drive
  • Free for 5 books, or $4.99 once to unlock unlimited
About Luma Reader Privacy Policy

AllNotes

OneNote nailed how notebooks should be organized—notebooks, section groups, and tabs—but its endless 2D canvas is annoying on a phone. AllNotes keeps that same notebook layout and puts everything on normal pages that wrap cleanly on any screen.

  • Headings, checklists, callout boxes, tables, and code blocks
  • Pages stay readable with zero sideways scrolling
  • Stored offline on your device with no sign-in required

Orbit Money

Keep track of accounts, monthly budgets, bills, and net worth without handing your spending history to an ad-funded app. Run it offline on your phone with fingerprint lock, or self-host it at home for your household and import your Rocket Money CSVs.

  • Exact to-the-cent math so totals never drift
  • Cleaning up messy bank merchant names never overwrites the original
  • Optional Docker server for shared household budgets and bank sync
