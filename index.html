Core apps

Built for long reading sessions, structured note-taking, and quiet personal finances without vendor lock-in.

Luma Reader

Read DRM-free EPUB books and PDF documents with customizable margins, line height, and reading themes. Use two-page spreads on larger screens, jump cleanly through chapters and bookmarks, or let Android text-to-speech read chapters aloud while your screen is locked.

  • Built on the official Readium 3.4.0 Kotlin toolkit
  • Syncs reading position & bookmarks in Google Drive drive.appdata
  • Free for up to 5 active books; single $4.99 Play Billing upgrade
Luma Reader details Privacy Policy

AllNotes

If you like how OneNote organizes Notebooks, Sections, and Section Groups, but miss clean document formatting and readable line widths, AllNotes puts both together. Everything lives offline in SQLDelight first, with a lightweight Ktor server when you want your notes on another device.

  • Rich structured blocks: checklists, callouts, code blocks, and tables
  • Bounded page width avoids horizontal scrolling on wide monitors
  • No third-party cloud custody — sync stays on your own server

Orbit Money

Keep track of accounts, category budgets, recurring expenses, and net worth without handing your financial life to an ad-funded aggregator. Orbit Money works offline on Android with biometric lock, or runs as a Docker container for household sharing and Rocket Money CSV imports.

  • Integer-cent arithmetic prevents floating-point rounding drift
  • Preserves raw merchant names alongside your cleaned labels
  • Optional encrypted SimpleFIN sync in self-hosted mode
