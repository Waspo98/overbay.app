Where your data lives

Every app keeps its database on your hardware first. Sync is optional and uses either your Google Drive app folder or a server you run.

App Platforms Default Storage Optional Sync Pricing & Source
Luma Reader app.overbay.lumareader Android Local library & Readium database Google Drive app folder (drive.appdata) 5 books free · $4.99 once · Closed source
AllNotes app.overbay.allnotes Android (Windows & Linux planned) Local SQLDelight SQLite database Self-hosted Ktor sync (planned) Free · Closed source

Core apps

Reading, structured notes, and personal finances without mandatory cloud accounts.

Luma Reader

Open DRM-free EPUB books and PDFs with adjustable margins, line height, and light, sepia, or dark themes. Read two-page spreads on foldables, track chapters and bookmarks, or listen with Android text-to-speech while your screen is locked.

  • Built on the official Readium 3.4.0 Kotlin toolkit
  • Syncs reading progress and bookmarks inside Google Drive drive.appdata
  • Free up to 5 active books, or $4.99 once via Play Billing
Luma Reader details Privacy Policy

AllNotes

OneNote nailed notebook organization—Notebooks, Section Groups, and Sections—but its infinite 2D canvas gets awkward on phones and wide monitors. I built AllNotes with the same notebook hierarchy and normal bounded pages that wrap properly on any screen.

  • Headings, interactive checklists, callouts, tables, and code blocks
  • Bounded column width prevents horizontal panning while writing
  • SQLDelight SQLite storage works offline with no account wall

Orbit Money

Track accounts, category budgets, recurring bills, and net worth without feeding your financial history to an ad-funded app. Run Orbit Money offline on Android behind biometric lock, or spin up the Docker container at home for shared household budgets and Rocket Money CSV imports.

  • Integer-cent math avoids floating-point rounding errors
  • Keeps raw original_* bank merchant strings next to your edited labels
  • Optional AES-256-GCM encrypted SimpleFIN sync in Docker mode

Overbay Labs: Open-source mods & tools

Alongside regular apps, I publish small Android utilities and clients under Overbay Labs on GitHub. These are distributed only as MIT open-source sideload APKs and never go on Google Play.

Overbay Labs projects are independent open-source tools and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by X Corp. or any third party.
