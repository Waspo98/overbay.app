Luma Reader

An Android EPUB and PDF reader built on Readium 3.4.0. Open your own books offline, adjust fonts and themes, listen with Android text-to-speech, and optionally sync reading progress via your private Google Drive app folder.

Inside the app

Screenshots from the current Android build across foldable and phone screens.

Two-page spread on foldables and tablets

On tablets and unfolded screens, Luma Reader displays facing pages side by side with page counts and chapter progress along the bottom.

Library & filters

Browse your local EPUB and PDF collection in a cover grid with instant search, reading-status filters, and author or series tags. Your books stay on your device without any storefront shelves.

Themes & accents

Pick System, Light, Sepia, Slate, or AMOLED dark mode alongside custom accent colors, with a live preview of how chapter text looks before you start reading. Inside the reader, you can also tune font family, size, line spacing, and margins.

Sync through your own Google Drive

Connect Google Drive during setup or anytime in Settings to keep reading positions, shelves, bookmarks, and theme preferences synced across devices. Luma Reader uses the private drive.appdata sandbox and cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Skip setup anytime

Setup takes four short screens, or tap Skip Setup at the top right to jump straight to your library and open a local EPUB or PDF file without signing into anything.

Why I built another reader

Most Android readers either wrap a bookstore storefront or lock basic progress sync behind a subscription. I built Luma Reader so my own DRM-free EPUBs and PDFs open fast, keep my page across a phone and foldable, and work with Android's built-in text-to-speech when I'm driving.

Instead of writing a fragile HTML parser from scratch, the reader is built on the Readium 3.4.0 Kotlin toolkit so footnotes, tables of contents, embedded fonts, and fixed-layout PDFs render reliably.

  • Readium 3.4.0 EPUB & PDF Engine

    Chapter navigation, bookmarks, cover extraction, and responsive single or dual-page rendering across phone, tablet, and foldable screens.

  • Background Android Read-Aloud (TTS)

    Uses Android's local system text-to-speech engine with foreground media controls and wake lock so narration keeps playing with your screen off.

  • Scoped Google Drive Sync

    Requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. Luma Reader cannot view or edit your personal Drive folders, photos, or documents.

  • Android Manifest Permissions

    INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK, and foreground service flags used only for Drive sync jobs and lock-screen audio controls.

Frequently asked questions

Does Luma Reader work completely offline?

Yes. Importing local EPUBs and PDFs, reading, bookmarking, and Android TTS narration work offline without network calls. Network access is only used if you turn on Google Drive sync or check Play Billing purchases.

What happens when I hit the 5-book limit on the free tier?

You keep full access to all reading features, themes, TTS, and Drive sync for up to 5 active books in your library. You can remove finished books anytime, or unlock an unlimited shelf permanently with a one-time $4.99 Play Billing purchase (SKU: luma_unlimited_lifetime).

Can Luma Reader read my other files on Google Drive?

No. Luma Reader requests only drive.appdata, which isolates the app to its own hidden application folder inside your Google Drive account.

Does Luma Reader open DRM-protected Kindle or Adobe books?

No. Luma Reader opens standard DRM-free EPUB and PDF files from your device storage.

How do I delete my data?

Clear app storage or uninstall Luma Reader to remove all local library records, revoke Luma Reader access in your Google Account third-party permissions if you connected Drive, or follow the Data Deletion guide.
