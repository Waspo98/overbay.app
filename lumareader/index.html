Inside the app

Screenshots from the Android app on phones and foldables.

Two-page spread on foldables and tablets

On tablets and unfolded screens, Luma Reader puts two pages side by side with page numbers and chapter progress along the bottom.

Library & filters

Browse your book covers in a grid with instant search, reading-status filters, and author or series tags. Just the books you loaded, with no bookstore tabs.

Themes & accents

Pick System, Light, Sepia, Slate, or AMOLED black along with custom accent colors and a live preview before you start reading. Inside a book, tweak font, size, line spacing, and margins.

Sync through your own Google Drive

Turn on Google Drive sync during setup or anytime in Settings to match your reading spot, shelves, bookmarks, and themes across devices. It only uses its own hidden app folder, so it can't see any of your other Drive files.

Skip setup anytime

Setup takes four quick screens, or tap Skip Setup in the top right to jump straight into your library without signing into anything.

Why I built another reader

Most Android readers are either bolted onto a bookstore or lock basic progress sync behind a monthly fee. I built Luma Reader so my own EPUBs and PDFs open fast, keep my page across a phone and foldable, and work with Android's built-in read-aloud when I'm driving.

It uses the open-source Readium engine, so footnotes, tables of contents, embedded fonts, and tricky PDFs render the way they should.

  • Solid EPUB & PDF rendering

    Smooth chapter jumping, bookmarks, cover extraction, and single or two-page layouts across phones, tablets, and foldables.

  • Read-aloud with your screen off

    Uses Android's built-in text-to-speech with lock-screen media controls so narration keeps playing while your screen is off.

  • Private Google Drive sync

    Only asks for its own hidden app folder (drive.appdata). Luma Reader can never view or touch your personal Drive folders, photos, or docs.

  • Minimal permissions

    Only asks for internet access (for optional Drive sync and Play purchases) and media notification controls for read-aloud.

Frequently asked questions

Does Luma Reader work offline?

Yes. Opening your EPUBs and PDFs, reading, bookmarks, and read-aloud all work offline. It only uses the internet if you turn on Google Drive sync or check a purchase.

How is Luma Reader different from Kindle, ReadEra, or Moon+ Reader?

Luma Reader skips bookstore feeds and ads, uses Readium 3.4 for EPUB and PDF rendering, shows two-page spreads on Android foldables and tablets, and limits Google Drive sync to Drive's hidden app-data folder so it can never see your other files.

What happens when I hit the 5-book free limit?

You get every feature (all themes, read-aloud, and Drive sync) for up to 5 active books. Remove finished books anytime to keep reading for free, or pay $4.99 once for an unlimited shelf. No subscriptions.

Can Luma Reader see my other files on Google Drive?

No. Luma Reader only asks for Google Drive's hidden app-data folder (drive.appdata), so it can't see any of your other Drive files.

Does Luma Reader open Kindle or copy-protected books?

No. Luma Reader opens standard DRM-free EPUB and PDF files stored on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Clear app storage or uninstall Luma Reader to wipe everything on your phone. If you turned on Google Drive sync, revoke access in your Google Account settings or follow my Data Deletion guide.
