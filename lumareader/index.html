Inside the app

Screenshots from the current Android build running across foldable and phone screen sizes.

Two-page spread on foldables and tablets

When unfolded or held horizontally on a tablet, Luma Reader lays out facing pages side by side with configurable column margins and a fast bottom scrubber for jumping across chapters.

Focused local library

Open the app straight to a Continue Reading card and a grid of cover thumbnails pulled directly from your EPUB and PDF files. No storefront carousels or book recommendations pushed onto your home tab.

Typography & theme controls

Adjust type size, line spacing, margins, alignment, and light, warm sepia, or dark themes without fighting tiny hidden menus. Settings apply cleanly across EPUB books via Readium stylesheets.

Sync through your own Google Drive

If you read across a phone and a tablet, sign into Google once and Luma Reader backs up reading positions and bookmarks inside Drive's hidden drive.appdata folder. There is no Overbay account server sitting in the middle.

5 books free, or $4.99 once for everything

Every feature — PDF support, read-aloud TTS, themes, and Drive sync — works on the free tier for up to 5 active books. Removing that shelf cap is a single $4.99 purchase through Google Play Billing.

No account wall before your first book

Pick any EPUB or PDF file from Android's document picker and start reading right away. Linking Google Drive is optional and tucked in settings whenever you want it.

Built around how reading actually works

Most mobile readers either tie you into their bookstore catalog or lock basic progress sync behind a yearly subscription. I built Luma Reader so my own DRM-free EPUBs and PDFs would open fast, remember my page across devices, and play nicely with Android's built-in text-to-speech when I'm driving.

Under the hood it uses the Readium 3.4.0 Kotlin toolkit rather than a fragile homebrew HTML parser, so footnotes, tables of contents, embedded fonts, and fixed-layout PDFs behave properly.

  • Readium 3.4.0 EPUB & PDF Engine

    Full chapter navigation, bookmarks, cover extraction, and responsive single or dual-page rendering across phone, tablet, and foldable screens.

  • Background Android Read-Aloud (TTS)

    Uses Android's local system text-to-speech engine with foreground media controls and wake lock so narration keeps playing with your screen off.

  • Least-Privilege Google Drive Sync

    Requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. Luma Reader never requests access to your personal Drive folders, photos, or documents.

  • Exact Manifest Permissions

    INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK, and foreground service flags strictly for Drive sync jobs and lock-screen audio controls.

Frequently asked questions

Does Luma Reader work completely offline?

Yes. Importing local EPUBs and PDFs, reading, bookmarking, and Android TTS narration work offline with zero network calls. Network access is only used if you choose to enable Google Drive sync or check Play Billing purchases.

What happens when I hit the 5-book limit on the free tier?

You keep full access to all reading features, themes, TTS, and Drive sync for up to 5 active books in your library. You can archive or remove books at any time, or unlock an unlimited shelf permanently for a one-time $4.99 Play Billing purchase (SKU: luma_unlimited_lifetime).

Can Luma Reader read my other files on Google Drive?

No. Luma Reader requests strictly drive.appdata, which isolates the app to its own hidden application folder inside your Google Drive account.

How do I delete my data?

Clear your app storage or uninstall Luma Reader to remove all local library records, revoke Luma Reader access in your Google Account third-party permissions if you connected Drive, or visit our Data Deletion guide.
