Why I built another Android browser

I love Zen Browser and Firefox on desktop, but Firefox on my 120Hz foldable always felt jittery when scrolling text. Chrome scrolls smoothly, yet gives you almost no control over ads, dark mode, or how tabs behave on a folding screen.

Swiftfox runs on Android's Chromium web view so scrolling stays smooth and security updates arrive through Google Play, while giving me the Space sidebar, ad blocking, and foldable controls I wanted.

  • Separate folded & unfolded layouts

    Keep one setup for your narrow cover screen (top, bottom, or split toolbar with mobile sites) and another when unfolded (a collapsible side rail with desktop sites). Folding doesn't reload open tabs or cut off audio unless the site mode switches.

  • Spaces, Essentials & Private tabs

    Group tabs into Zen-style Spaces with pinned Essentials. Private tabs clear their cookies and storage as soon as you close them, and you can optionally keep cookies isolated per Space.

  • Built-in ad-block & Element Zapper

    Ships with EasyList, EasyPrivacy, and uBlock Origin filter lists, plus custom list URLs. When a sticky banner or cookie overlay slips past, tap Element Zapper to hide it with undo or save a permanent rule for that site.

  • Dark Reader, userscripts & Reader Mode

    Turn on Dark Reader for websites independently of your phone theme (with true black AMOLED and auto-skip for sites that already have dark mode), add .user.js scripts, set per-site overrides, or switch into Reader Mode.

Sync, everyday tools, and limits

Sign into your Mozilla Account in the app or scan a QR code from Desktop Firefox or Zen Browser to sync bookmarks, history, open tabs, and Zen Spaces and Essentials. Switching from Chrome? Import or export a standard HTML bookmark file locally anytime.

The day-to-day basics are here too: switchable search engines (Google, DuckDuckGo, Kagi, Brave, Startpage, or custom URLs with !bang shortcuts), Picture-in-Picture, background audio, clean URL sharing, HTTPS-Only mode, and Android autofill.

Technical notes & limits

  • Engine: Android's system Chromium web view, updated automatically through Google Play.
  • Extensions: Chromium web view can't run .crx or .xpi extensions, so Swiftfox relies on built-in ad-block, Element Zapper, Dark Reader, .user.js scripts, and per-site rules instead.
  • Passwords: Uses Android System Autofill and Credential Manager rather than a separate password vault.
  • Themes: Day, Dark, and true black AMOLED chrome with Fox Amber, Midnight Indigo, Forest, Monochrome, or Material You accents.

Frequently asked questions

Why use Android's Chromium web view instead of GeckoView or a custom Chromium fork?

On 120Hz Android screens, GeckoView text scrolling often jitters compared to Chromium. Maintaining a full Chromium fork takes huge C++ builds and constant security patching. Using Android's built-in Chromium web view gives me smooth 120Hz scrolling and automatic Google Play security updates, so I can put my time into the browser UI, built-in ad-block, Dark Reader, and sync.

Can Swiftfox install Chrome or Firefox extensions?

No. Android's Chromium web view doesn't support .crx or .xpi browser extensions. That's why I built the tools I actually use straight into Swiftfox: built-in ad-block with uBlock Origin and EasyList filters, Element Zapper, Dark Reader, per-site rules, and .user.js userscripts.

How does Firefox and Zen Browser sync work?

Sign in with your Mozilla Account inside the app or scan a QR code from Desktop Firefox or Zen Browser. Swiftfox talks directly to Mozilla's sync servers to keep your bookmarks, history, open tabs, Zen Spaces, and Essentials up to date.

Does folding or unfolding reload my open tabs?

No, unless your folded and unfolded setups pick a different Mobile or Desktop mode for that site. Open tabs stay loaded and playing audio keeps going when you open or close your phone.

How are passwords and autofill handled?

Swiftfox hands logins and passkeys off to Android System Autofill and Credential Manager, so whatever password manager you already use just works.

Does Swiftfox send my browsing history to Overbay.app?

No. I don't run any Swiftfox servers or analytics. Your tabs, Spaces, history, and rules stay on your phone. If you turn on Mozilla Sync, your device talks directly to Mozilla's servers.
