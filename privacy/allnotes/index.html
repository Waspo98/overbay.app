AllNotes Privacy Policy

Package: app.overbay.allnotes Developer: Overbay Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Contact: privacy@overbay.app
Summary: AllNotes stores all notebooks, sections, and pages in a local SQLDelight SQLite database on your device. Overbay operates no central note cloud, runs zero analytics or ads, and transmits notes only if you point the app at a self-hosted Ktor sync server of your own.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

AllNotes stores your workspaces, notebooks, section groups, sections, pages, checklists, callout blocks, tables, code blocks, and server configuration settings (stored in local workspace_server.json and SQLite files) solely inside private application storage on your device.

2. Optional Self-Hosted Sync Server

By default, AllNotes operates completely offline without making network requests. If you manually configure a sync endpoint URL (with an optional bearer token or OIDC reverse-proxy login header), the app sends your note delta payloads directly to the server URL you entered. Overbay does not operate public AllNotes hosting or view data stored on user-operated servers.

3. Declared Android Permissions

AllNotes declares only one sensitive system capability in its Android manifest:

  • INTERNET: Used strictly when you explicitly link a self-hosted AllNotes sync server URL in Workspace Settings.

AllNotes does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or advertising identifier permissions.

4. Analytics, Advertising & Data Sharing

AllNotes contains zero analytics SDKs, zero ad networks, and zero crash-telemetry trackers. Overbay does not collect, sell, rent, or transfer your note contents or personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your notebooks remain on your device until you delete pages in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall AllNotes. If you configured a self-hosted server, removing synced notes is controlled on that server. Full instructions are listed on our Data Deletion page.

6. Children's Privacy

AllNotes is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

Any material changes to permissions or sync behavior will update this policy at https://overbay.app/privacy/allnotes/ prior to shipping. Questions may be sent to privacy@overbay.app.
