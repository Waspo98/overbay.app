Privacy Policy

Developer / Publisher: Neal Overbay Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Contact: privacy@overbay.app
Summary: My apps keep your books, notes, and finances on your own device by default. I don't run ads, tracking, or user accounts.

1. Per-App Google Play Privacy Policies

Each app I publish on Google Play has its own privacy policy covering its exact permissions, Google Drive scopes, and storage:

  • Luma Reader (app.overbay.lumareader): overbay.app/privacy/lumareader/
  • AllNotes (app.overbay.allnotes): overbay.app/privacy/allnotes/
  • Orbit Money (app.overbay.orbit): overbay.app/privacy/orbitmoney/
  • Data & Account Deletion Instructions (all apps): overbay.app/data-deletion/

2. Website Privacy (overbay.app)

https://overbay.app is a static website. I don't set tracking cookies, embed ads, run analytics scripts, or load external fonts.

Standard server and CDN logs (requested paths, timestamps, HTTP status codes, and temporary IP addresses needed for HTTPS and basic abuse protection) are handled automatically by my hosting provider and are never tied to any app data.

3. Summary of Luma Reader Privacy (In-App Link Reference)

Older builds of Luma Reader link directly to this page from the in-app About menu. Here is the summary of the full Luma Reader Privacy Policy:

  • Local Library: Your EPUB and PDF files, cover thumbnails, bookmarks, and settings stay on your Android device.
  • Optional Google Drive Sync: If you turn on Drive sync, Luma Reader requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope, restricting it to its own hidden app-data folder for syncing reading positions and bookmarks. It can never view or change your other Google Drive files.
  • Google API Limited Use Compliance: Luma Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for ads, profiling, or sold to third parties.
  • Play Billing: The optional one-time luma_unlimited_lifetime ($4.99) upgrade is handled by Google Play Billing. I never see your credit card or payment details.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

I never sell, rent, or trade your data. My apps only make network connections if you turn on an optional feature—like signing into Google Drive in Luma Reader or Orbit Money, pointing AllNotes at your own sync server, or checking a Play Billing purchase via Google Play.

5. Children's Privacy

My apps and this website are meant for a general audience and aren't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). I don't knowingly collect personal info from children.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because I don't run central user account servers, your data stays on your phone, in your own Google Drive app folder or backups if enabled, or on servers you host yourself. See the Data Deletion guide to wipe local data, revoke Google permissions, or ask me to delete support emails.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

If permissions or third-party features change in a future release, I'll update this page and the matching per-app policy before shipping it. Questions or deletion requests can go to privacy@overbay.app.
