Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2025

Overbay ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Luma Reader mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

The short version: We collect as little data as possible. Your books and reading data stay on your device or in your own Google Drive account. We don't run any servers that store your data.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Google Account Email: When you enable Google Drive cloud sync, you sign in with your Google account. We receive your email address solely to identify your sync account within the app. This email is displayed locally in the app and is never transmitted to any server we operate.
  • Purchase Information: If you purchase the Unlimited Library upgrade, Google Play handles the entire transaction. We receive a purchase token to verify your purchase status but never see your payment details (credit card, billing address, etc.).

Information stored on your device:

  • Your EPUB book files
  • Reading positions, bookmarks, annotations, and highlights
  • Library organization (shelves, collections)
  • App preferences and settings

This data is stored locally on your device and is not accessible to us.

2. Google Drive Cloud Sync

When you enable cloud sync, Luma Reader uses the Google Drive Application Data folder (appDataFolder) to store your reading data. This is a special hidden folder in your Google Drive that:

  • Is only accessible by Luma Reader — no other apps or users can see it
  • Does not appear in your visible Google Drive files
  • Stores your reading positions, bookmarks, library catalog, preferences, and optionally your EPUB files

We use the following Google API scopes:

  • drive.appdata — Access to the hidden application data folder only
  • drive.file — Access only to files created by the app

We never access your personal Google Drive files, documents, photos, or any data outside the app's private folder.

You can disconnect sync and wipe all remote data at any time from within the app's sync settings.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics
  • We do not use tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or fingerprinting
  • We do not collect device identifiers for advertising
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any personal data to third parties
  • We do not operate any servers that receive or store your data

4. Third-Party Services

Luma Reader integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services (Authentication): Used for Google Sign-In when enabling cloud sync. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Drive API: Used exclusively for cloud sync via the Application Data folder. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: Used for in-app purchases. Google handles all payment processing. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security

All communication between Luma Reader and Google's APIs occurs over HTTPS (TLS-encrypted connections). Your local data is stored in the app's private storage area on your Android device, protected by the Android operating system's sandboxing.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local data: Deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data.
  • Cloud data: You can wipe all synced data from Google Drive at any time using the "Wipe Remote Data" option in the app's sync settings. You can also revoke Luma Reader's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children's Privacy

Luma Reader is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
