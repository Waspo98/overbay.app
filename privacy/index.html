Privacy Policy

Developer / Publisher: Overbay Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Contact: privacy@overbay.app
Summary: Overbay apps keep your books, notes, and ledger files locally on your device by default. Overbay runs no analytics, no third-party ad SDKs, and no central user account servers.

1. Per-App Google Play Privacy Policies

Each Google Play application published by Overbay has a dedicated, package-specific privacy policy covering its permissions, OAuth scopes, and storage model:

  • Luma Reader (app.overbay.lumareader): overbay.app/privacy/lumareader/
  • AllNotes (app.overbay.allnotes): overbay.app/privacy/allnotes/
  • Orbit Money (app.overbay.orbit): overbay.app/privacy/orbitmoney/
  • Data & Account Deletion Instructions (all apps): overbay.app/data-deletion/

2. Website Privacy (overbay.app)

The website https://overbay.app consists of static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Overbay does not set tracking cookies, embed third-party ad networks, run analytics scripts, or load external font CDNs.

Standard web server and CDN edge delivery logs (requested paths, timestamps, HTTP status codes, and transient IP addresses needed for TLS and abuse protection) are handled automatically at the network edge and are never linked to in-app data.

3. Summary of Luma Reader Privacy (In-App Link Reference)

Older builds of Luma Reader link directly to this page from the in-app About menu. The full Luma Reader Privacy Policy is summarized below:

  • Local Library: Your EPUB and PDF files, cover thumbnails, bookmarks, and preferences stay in local Android storage on your device.
  • Optional Google Drive Sync: If you enable Drive sync, Luma Reader requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope, limiting access to its own hidden app-data folder for syncing reading positions and bookmarks. It cannot inspect or modify your general Google Drive files.
  • Google API Limited Use Compliance: Luma Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.
  • Play Billing: The optional one-time luma_unlimited_lifetime ($4.99) upgrade is processed through Google Play Billing. Overbay never receives your credit card or payment instrument details.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Overbay does not sell, rent, or trade user personal data. External network connections occur only when you enable an optional feature (signing into Google Drive in Luma Reader or Orbit Money, pointing AllNotes at a self-hosted sync server, or checking Play Billing entitlements via Google Play).

5. Children's Privacy

Overbay applications and this website are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Overbay does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Overbay does not operate central user account servers, data resides inside your device storage, inside your own Google Drive app folder or snapshots if enabled, or on servers you self-host. Follow the Data Deletion guide to wipe local data, revoke Google OAuth tokens, or request removal of email support correspondence.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

If permissions or third-party integrations change in a future release, Overbay will update this page and the affected per-app privacy policy before shipping the release. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to privacy@overbay.app.
