Orbit Money Privacy Policy

Package: app.overbay.orbit Developer: Overbay Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Contact: privacy@overbay.app
Summary: Orbit Money keeps your accounts, budgets, and transactions on your own phone or your own self-hosted Docker server. Overbay does not host user financial databases, collect behavioral telemetry, or sell financial records.

1. Data Stored Locally vs. Self-Hosted Mode

Orbit Money works in two mutually independent ways chosen by you:

  • On-Device Local Mode (Android & PWA): Account balances, categories, recurring bills, and transaction history are stored locally on your device. If you enable Biometric Lock, authentication uses Android's hardware-backed BiometricPrompt; raw fingerprint or face templates never enter Orbit Money or leave Android's keystore.
  • Self-Hosted Household Mode (Docker + SQLite): When you connect the app to a self-hosted Orbit Money Docker instance you operate (using the orbitmoney:// scheme or HTTPS URL), financial ledger entries and authentication state reside inside your own server's SQLite volume (AUTH_PROVIDER=local|oidc|both). Overbay cannot access servers you host.

2. Optional Google Drive Snapshot Sync

In standalone Android mode, you may optionally sign in with Google to store and restore ledger snapshots in your own Google Drive account. Depending on whether you use hidden app sync or visible snapshot export, Orbit Money requests only:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — restricted to Orbit Money's hidden application-specific Drive folder.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — restricted strictly to Orbit Money backup files created or explicitly opened by the app.

Orbit Money never requests access to read unrelated documents, spreadsheets, or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Orbit Money's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Google Drive access is used solely to save and restore your own Orbit Money ledger snapshots inside your own Google Drive account.
  • Google user data is never sold, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or used for advertising, underwriting, or model training.
  • Overbay developers never view or read your ledger snapshots stored in Google Drive.

4. Optional Third-Party Integrations (Self-Hosted Mode)

Certain optional integrations are available only when explicitly configured by the administrator of a self-hosted instance:

  • SimpleFIN Bridge: If you provide a SimpleFIN Setup Token / Access URL, your self-hosted server stores the Access URL AES-256-GCM encrypted in SQLite and queries SimpleFIN directly on your schedule. Overbay does not relay SimpleFIN tokens.
  • Logo.dev Merchant Icons (Opt-In): If configured, merchant domain lookups fetch brand icons directly from Logo.dev.
  • Web Push Alerts (Opt-In): Self-hosted administrators may optionally configure VAPID keys for browser push alerts.

5. Declared Android Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required only when enabling Google Drive snapshots or connecting to a self-hosted Docker server URL.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Used solely for optional local app unlock via Android's system biometric prompt.
  • VIBRATE: Used for tactile UI confirmation in numeric entry and navigation.

6. Non-Financial-Institution Notice

Orbit Money is personal bookkeeping software only. It does not hold funds, execute bank transfers, extend credit, provide tax advice, or act as a bank or registered financial advisor.

7. Analytics, Retention & Deletion

Orbit Money contains zero third-party ads or behavioral analytics SDKs. Remove your data at any time via the in-app reset, Android Clear Storage/Uninstall, Google Drive permission revocation, or deleting your self-hosted Docker /data volume. Full steps appear on our Data Deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy & Contact

Orbit Money is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Questions or privacy requests may be emailed to privacy@overbay.app.
