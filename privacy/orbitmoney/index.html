Orbit Money Privacy Policy

Package: app.overbay.orbit Developer: Neal Overbay Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Contact: privacy@overbay.app
Summary: Orbit Money keeps your accounts, budgets, and transactions on your phone or on your own self-hosted Docker server. I don't host user financial databases, collect tracking data, or sell your records.

1. Data Stored Locally vs. Self-Hosted Mode

Orbit Money works in two separate modes chosen by you:

  • On-Device Local Mode (Android & PWA): Account balances, categories, recurring bills, and transactions stay locally on your phone or browser. If you turn on Biometric Lock, unlocking uses Android's hardware-backed biometric prompt; your fingerprint or face data never enters Orbit Money or leaves Android's secure keystore.
  • Self-Hosted Household Mode (Docker + SQLite): When you connect the app to a self-hosted Orbit Money Docker server you run (via orbitmoney:// or HTTPS), your ledger and login data stay inside your server's SQLite folder (AUTH_PROVIDER=local|oidc|both). I can't see or access servers you host.

2. Optional Google Drive Backup Sync

In standalone Android mode, you can optionally sign in with Google to back up and restore your ledger in your Google Drive account. Depending on whether you use hidden app sync or visible backup exports, Orbit Money asks only for:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — restricted to Orbit Money's hidden app-data folder in Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — restricted to Orbit Money backup files created or opened directly by the app.

Orbit Money never asks to view or touch any of your other documents, spreadsheets, or folders in Google Drive.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Orbit Money's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Google Drive access is used solely to back up and restore your own Orbit Money data inside your Google Drive account.
  • Google user data is never sold, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or used for ads, credit underwriting, or training AI models.
  • I never read or view backups stored in your Google Drive.

4. Optional Third-Party Integrations (Self-Hosted Mode)

A few optional extras are only active when explicitly turned on by whoever runs a self-hosted instance:

  • SimpleFIN Bridge: If you add a SimpleFIN Setup Token or Access URL, your self-hosted server encrypts the Access URL inside SQLite and fetches updates directly from SimpleFIN. I never see or relay SimpleFIN tokens.
  • Logo.dev Merchant Icons (Opt-In): If turned on, merchant domain lookups fetch brand icons directly from Logo.dev.
  • Web Push Alerts (Opt-In): Self-hosted admins can optionally set up VAPID keys for browser push notifications.

5. Declared Android Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used only when you turn on Google Drive backups or connect to a self-hosted Docker server.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Used only for optional fingerprint or face unlock via Android's system prompt.
  • VIBRATE: Used for haptic feedback when tapping buttons and entering numbers.

6. Not a Bank or Financial Advisor

Orbit Money is personal budgeting software only. It does not hold money, move funds, offer credit, give tax advice, or act as a bank or financial advisor.

7. Analytics, Retention & Deletion

Orbit Money has no ads or analytics trackers. Remove your data anytime using the in-app reset, Android's Clear Storage or Uninstall options, revoking Google Drive access, or deleting your self-hosted Docker /data folder. Full steps live on the Data Deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy & Contact

Orbit Money isn't intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and I don't knowingly collect personal info from children. Questions or privacy requests? Email me at privacy@overbay.app.
