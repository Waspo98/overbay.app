Swiftfox Privacy Policy

Package: app.overbay.swiftfox Developer: Neal Overbay Effective Date: November 24, 2026 Contact: privacy@overbay.app
Summary: Swiftfox keeps your tabs, Spaces, bookmarks, history, userscripts, and site rules on your own phone. I don't run Swiftfox servers, proxy your traffic, or collect browsing analytics. Optional Firefox and Zen Browser sync connects directly to Mozilla's servers.

1. Local-First Browsing Data & Profile Isolation

Swiftfox stores your browser state locally inside private app storage on your Android device (with android:allowBackup="false" disabled for system cloud backups):

  • Local Database & Preferences: Spaces, pinned Essentials, open tabs, bookmarks, history, downloads, per-site rules, Element Zapper selectors, installed .user.js scripts, and foldable posture layouts stay locally in Swiftfox's Room database and DataStore preferences.
  • Private Tabs & Container Isolation: Private tabs use an ephemeral Android WebView ProfileStore session (swiftfox_private_session) whose cookies, cache, and storage are wiped when Private browsing ends. Optional per-Space cookie containers (space_<id>) keep cookies isolated per Space on your device.
  • Passwords & Autofill: Swiftfox delegates saved logins and passkeys to Android System Autofill and Credential Manager rather than storing passwords in an in-app vault.

2. Direct Web Connections, Search & Filter Updates

When you browse the web in Swiftfox, connections go directly from Android's system Chromium WebView to the sites you visit—there is no Overbay.app middleman server or proxy.

  • Search & Suggestions: Queries and optional live search suggestions are sent only to whichever search engine you select in Settings (Google by default, or DuckDuckGo, Kagi, Brave, Startpage, or a custom URL you enter).
  • Adblock Lists & Userscripts: Periodic filter list updates for adblock-rust (such as EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock Origin lists, or custom list URLs you add) and any .user.js scripts or GM_xmlhttpRequest calls fetch directly from their configured URLs.

3. Optional Firefox & Zen Browser Sync (Mozilla Accounts)

Sync is completely off by default. If you choose to sign in with a Mozilla Account (via the isolated in-app OAuth WebView, QR code pairing, or pairing link):

  • Swiftfox authenticates directly with Mozilla's Firefox Accounts and Sync 1.5 servers using Mozilla application-services to sync bookmarks, history, synced open tabs, and Zen Spaces/Essentials/pinned items.
  • I never receive your Mozilla credentials, sync keys, or synced records. Data stored in your Mozilla Account is governed by Mozilla's Privacy Notice.
  • Chrome Netscape HTML bookmark import and export operate strictly offline using local files you pick.

4. Declared Android Permissions

Swiftfox declares only the permissions needed for browser functionality and site features you explicitly use:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to load websites, check connectivity, fetch filter list updates, and sync if you sign into a Mozilla Account.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Keeps background audio and media controls running when playing media from a webpage with the screen off or in Picture-in-Picture.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Shows download progress, background media playback controls, and optional site notifications you allow.
  • CAMERA (runtime permission): Used only when you open QR code device pairing for Mozilla Sync or grant camera access to a specific website.
  • RECORD_AUDIO & MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS (runtime permission): Used only when you grant microphone access to a specific website (such as voice calls).
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (runtime permission): Used only when you grant location access to a specific website.

5. Analytics, Ads & Data Sharing

Swiftfox contains zero ads, ad SDKs, crash reporters, or usage telemetry. I never collect, view, sell, rent, or share your browsing history, bookmarks, or personal data.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You can clear browsing history, cookies, site storage, or Private sessions inside Swiftfox anytime, sign out of Mozilla Sync in Settings > Sync (and unlink the device at accounts.firefox.com), or wipe everything locally via Android Settings > Apps > Clear storage or uninstall. Full instructions live on the Data Deletion page.

7. Trademarks, Children's Privacy & Contact

Swiftfox is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). Questions? Email me at privacy@overbay.app.
