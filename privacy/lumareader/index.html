Luma Reader Privacy Policy

Package: app.overbay.lumareader Developer: Overbay Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Contact: privacy@overbay.app
Summary: Luma Reader opens EPUB and PDF files locally on your Android device. Overbay collects no usage analytics, sells no data, and only connects to Google Drive if you choose to enable sync using Drive's isolated app-data scope.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

When you open EPUB or PDF files in Luma Reader via Android's document picker, the app stores only what it needs inside its private local storage:

  • Imported copies or references to your EPUB and PDF publications and extracted cover thumbnails.
  • Local SQLite metadata covering your reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, and typography or theme preferences.
  • Read-aloud state processed on-device via Readium 3.4.0 and your device's built-in Android text-to-speech engine.

Overbay has no access to the books you open or read on your device.

2. Optional Google Drive App-Folder Sync

Signing in with Google is optional. If you turn on cross-device sync in Settings, Luma Reader authenticates with Google Sign-In and requests only the following OAuth 2.0 scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This scope restricts Luma Reader exclusively to its own hidden application folder inside your Google Drive account to synchronize reading progress, bookmarks, and library sync metadata across your own Android devices. Luma Reader never requests access to view, edit, or list any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Luma Reader's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Google Drive API access is used solely to store and retrieve your own Luma Reader reading progress and bookmarks inside your private drive.appdata folder.
  • Google user data is never transferred or sold to third parties, advertising networks, data brokers, or credit agencies.
  • Google user data is never used for targeted advertising, personalized marketing, or training machine learning or AI models.
  • Overbay developers do not view or inspect data inside your Google Drive app folder.

4. Google Play Billing

Luma Reader is free for up to 5 active books and offers an optional one-time in-app upgrade (luma_unlimited_lifetime, $4.99 USD equivalent) processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Overbay receives only non-sensitive entitlement status and purchase tokens required to unlock unlimited library capacity; Google processes and retains payment card and billing details under Google's Terms and Privacy Policy.

5. Android Permissions Explained

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used only when you connect optional Google Drive sync or query Google Play Billing entitlements.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Used to display active read-aloud TTS notification controls, keep audio playback running while the screen is locked, and finish user-initiated Drive sync jobs.

6. Analytics, Advertising & Crash Telemetry

Luma Reader contains no third-party ad SDKs, analytics trackers, or crash-telemetry libraries.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Local reading records persist on your device until you remove a book, clear app storage, or uninstall Luma Reader. Drive app-folder sync files remain in your own Google Drive until you delete them or disconnect access. Full deletion steps are listed on the Data Deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy

Luma Reader is not directed at children under age 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and Overbay does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes & Contact

Updates to this policy will be published at https://overbay.app/privacy/lumareader/ with a revised effective date. Contact the developer anytime at privacy@overbay.app.
