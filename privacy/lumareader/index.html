Luma Reader Privacy Policy

Package: app.overbay.lumareader Developer: Neal Overbay Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Contact: privacy@overbay.app
Summary: Luma Reader opens EPUB and PDF files offline on your Android device. I don't collect analytics or sell data, and the app only connects to Google Drive if you choose to turn on sync using Drive's hidden app folder.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

When you open EPUB or PDF files in Luma Reader through Android's file picker, the app keeps only what it needs in private local storage:

  • Imported copies or references to your EPUB and PDF files along with extracted cover images.
  • A local database storing your reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, and font or theme choices.
  • Read-aloud progress handled on your device using Android's built-in text-to-speech.

I have zero access to the books you load or read on your device.

2. Optional Google Drive App-Folder Sync

Signing in with Google is completely optional. If you turn on cross-device sync in Settings, Luma Reader signs in with Google and asks only for this OAuth 2.0 scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

This limits Luma Reader strictly to its own hidden application folder in your Google Drive to sync reading positions, bookmarks, and shelf data between your own Android devices. Luma Reader never asks to view, edit, or list any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Luma Reader's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Google Drive access is used solely to save and restore your own Luma Reader reading progress and bookmarks inside your private drive.appdata folder.
  • Google user data is never transferred or sold to third parties, ad networks, data brokers, or credit agencies.
  • Google user data is never used for targeted ads, marketing, or training AI/machine learning models.
  • I never view or read anything inside your Google Drive app folder.

4. Google Play Billing

Luma Reader is free for up to 5 active books and offers an optional one-time in-app upgrade (luma_unlimited_lifetime, $4.99 USD equivalent) handled completely by Google Play Billing. I only receive the purchase status and tokens needed to unlock your shelf; Google handles all payment card and billing details under Google's Terms and Privacy Policy.

5. Android Permissions Explained

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used only when you turn on optional Google Drive sync or check a Google Play purchase.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Used to show read-aloud media controls, keep audio narration playing while your screen is locked, and finish Drive sync jobs you start.

6. Analytics, Ads & Tracking

Luma Reader includes no third-party ad networks, analytics trackers, or crash-reporting libraries.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your local library stays on your phone until you remove a book, clear app storage, or uninstall Luma Reader. Drive sync files stay in your own Google Drive until you delete them or disconnect access. Full steps live on the Data Deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy

Luma Reader isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and I don't knowingly collect personal info from children.

9. Changes & Contact

I'll post any updates to this policy at https://overbay.app/privacy/lumareader/ with a new effective date. Questions? Email me at privacy@overbay.app.
