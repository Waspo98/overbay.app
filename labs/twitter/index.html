How timeline filtering works

Hiding sponsored posts with CSS alone leaves blank gaps in the feed and still executes ad scripts. Instead, this wrapper intercepts GraphQL JSON responses (like HomeTimeline, UserTweets, and search feeds) on your device and removes promoted entries before the WebView renders them.

Everything runs locally inside Android WebView and OkHttp. Your session cookies stay on your phone and connect directly to X/Twitter without a proxy server or analytics.

  • GraphQL Ad Pruning

    Removes promoted posts and inline suggestion modules from API payloads before layout runs, avoiding empty cards and scroll jumps.

  • Classic Blue & Original Labels

    Optional styling restores #1DA1F2 accents alongside "Tweet" and "Retweet" labels if you prefer the older Twitter interface.

  • t.co Unwrapping & Regex Mute Filters

    Rewrites short tracking URLs directly to their destination when available and lets you mute topics or domains locally with regular expressions.

  • Direct Media Downloads

    Save highest-bitrate .mp4 videos and original-resolution (name=orig) images straight to your Android media library.

Questions & build notes

Why is this under Overbay Labs instead of Google Play?

Third-party web wrappers for someone else's platform don't belong on Google Play. Publishing it under Overbay Labs with MIT source lets you read every line of Kotlin and JavaScript yourself and sideload the APK if you want it.

Does this app collect my login credentials or timeline data?

No. Login happens directly inside Android WebView against X's own login flow. I run no servers for this app and include no crash reporters or analytics SDKs.
