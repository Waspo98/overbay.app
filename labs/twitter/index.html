How filtering works cleanly

Hiding sponsored tweets only with CSS leaves blank vertical gaps and lets tracking scripts run first. Instead, Overbay Labs intercepts GraphQL JSON payloads on-device (such as HomeTimeline, UserTweets, and search feeds) before the WebView renders them, stripping items containing promoted metadata cleanly from the feed array.

The entire wrapper runs locally on your phone. Your session cookies stay inside standard Android WebView storage, talking straight to X/Twitter without any proxy server or third-party analytics.

  • Network-Level GraphQL Pruning

    Filters promoted posts and inline suggestion modules out of API responses before layout runs, avoiding timeline stutter and empty placeholder cards.

  • Classic Blue & Terminologies

    Optional classic styling restores #1DA1F2 accents alongside "Tweet" and "Retweet" strings if you prefer the older Twitter interface.

  • t.co Unwrapping & Regex Mute Filters

    Rewrites short tracking URLs directly to their destination when available and lets you mute unwanted topics or domains locally with regular expressions.

  • Direct Media Downloads

    Save highest-bitrate .mp4 videos and original-resolution (name=orig) images directly into your Android media library.

Questions & build notes

Why is this under Overbay Labs instead of Google Play?

Modified third-party client wrappers belong outside Google Play for clear policy and trademark boundaries. Publishing it under Overbay Labs on MIT source lets you inspect every line of Kotlin and JavaScript yourself and install the APK directly if you want it.

Does this app collect my login credentials or timeline data?

No. Login happens directly inside Android WebView against X's own login flow. Overbay operates no servers for this app and includes no crash reporters or analytics SDKs.
