What it actually fixes

Just hiding sponsored posts with CSS leaves awkward blank gaps in your timeline and still loads the ad junk anyway. Instead, this app removes promoted posts from the feed before they appear on screen—so your timeline scrolls smoothly with zero empty cards.

Everything runs locally on your phone. Your login stays inside Android's browser view and connects straight to X with no middleman server and no tracking.

  • No ads in your feed

    Strips promoted posts and suggestion clutter before the timeline renders, so you never scroll past blank gaps.

  • No Grok or Premium nagging

    Hides Grok buttons and constant Subscribe/Premium upsells, and lets you choose which home tabs show up.

  • Classic blue & original labels

    Optional styling brings back Twitter blue (#1DA1F2) along with "Tweet" and "Retweet" labels if you miss the old look.

  • Clean links, mute filters & media downloads

    Skips tracking redirects automatically, lets you mute keywords locally, and saves full-resolution photos and MP4 videos straight to your phone.

Questions & build notes

Why isn't this on Google Play?

Third-party wrappers for someone else's site don't belong on Google Play. Putting it under Overbay Labs with MIT open-source code on GitHub lets anyone check every line and sideload the APK if they want it.

Does this app collect my login or timeline data?

No. You sign in straight on X's own login page inside the app. There are no Overbay servers, no crash reporters, and no analytics.
